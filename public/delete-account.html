Delete your account

Time and Time Again

You can delete your Time and Time Again account and everything stored in it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

From inside the app — the fastest way

  1. Open Time and Time Again and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account, then confirm with Delete everything.

Your account and all of your Things are removed immediately. If the app asks you to sign in again first, that is a safety check on old sessions — sign out, sign back in, and repeat.

By email — if you cannot sign in

Email jeeholife2@gmail.com from the address you registered with, asking for your account to be deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.

The request must come from the registered address — it is the only way to confirm the account is yours.

What is deleted, and what is not

Your account record Deleted immediately
All of your Things — titles, notes, times, tags Deleted immediately
Your stored Google authorization, if you connected one Deleted immediately; no further writes to your calendar are possible
Copies held in routine backups Removed within 30 days
Events and tasks already written to your Google Calendar or Google Tasks Not deleted. Those belong to your Google account. Delete them from Google Calendar or Google Tasks directly

The app keeps no other copy of your data, and there is nothing retained for advertising or analytics — none is collected in the first place. See the privacy policy.
